BLUE GREEN LIFESTYLES

WHAT IS BLUE GREEN LIFESTYLE? We are putting together a community of of health and wellness professionals with the same goals. Discovering and sharing all lifestyle changes needed to live a healthy life past the age of 100. Mankind has always searched for the proverbial ‘fountain of youth’, but science today has discovered that its not just genes. It’s a combination of many things. I’m sure you may have heard of the Blue Zones? There are five recognized regions in the world, often called Blue Zones , where people live the longest and healthiest lives. Suggested blue zones include Okinawa Prefecture in Japan , Nuoro Province in Sardinia, Italy, the Nicoya Peninsula in Costa Rica, and Icaria, Greece. The question is if these environments can be reproduced where we live. We believe it can, and with our new Blue Green Lifestyle community of 50 great minds and researchers, the goal is to put together a road map and lifestyle to make this a possibility globally. As I write this, I am in rehab for a broken hip. I was lying on a stretcher in a bright, cold, noisy emergency hospital hallway for 3 days and nights before I could get the needed surgery. I had never experienced anything like this, and I never want anyone to go through this. But this experience reminded me how fragile we are, and precious our health is. I never want to see the inside of a hospital again. I have always known about the importance of ‘preventive maintenance’. We don’t wait for our car engine to seize before getting an oil change. Right Tribe The world’s longest lived people chose–or were born into–social circles that supported healthy behaviors, Okinawans created ”moais”–groups of five friends that committed to each other for life. Research from the Framingham Studies shows that smoking, obesity, happiness, and even loneliness are contagious. So the social networks of long-lived people have favorably shaped their health behaviors.

LOVED ONES

We all know the importance of family. But not everyone has a loving or functional family. So we invite you to be part of the BlueGreen Lifestyle family. Loved Ones First Successful centenarians in the blue zones put their families first. This means keeping aging parents and grandparents nearby or in the home (It lowers disease and mortality rates of children in the home too.). They commit to a life partner (which can add up to 3 years of life expectancy) and invest in their children with time and love (They’ll be more likely to care for you when the time comes).

RIGHT TRIBE Again ‘We are who we associate with’ Belong All but five of the 263 centenarians we interviewed belonged to some faith-based community. Denomination doesn’t seem to matter. Research shows that attending faith-based services four times per month will add 4-14 years of life expectancy.
