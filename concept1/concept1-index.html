About Blue Green Lifestyle

Mankind has always searched for the proverbial ‘fountain of youth’, but science today has discovered that its not just genes. It’s a combination of many things. There are five recognized regions in the world, often called Blue Zones, where people live the longest and healthiest lives: Okinawa Prefecture in Japan, Nuoro Province in Sardinia, Italy, the Nicoya Peninsula in Costa Rica, and Icaria, Greece. The question is if these environments can be reproduced where we live. We believe it can.

The 9 Principles

1. Move Naturally

The world’s longest-lived people live in environments that nudge them to move naturally.

2. Purpose

Knowing why you wake up in the morning adds healthy years to your life.

3. Downshift

Stress leads to chronic inflammation — the longest-lived people have routines that shed it.

4. 80% Rule

Stop eating when your stomach is 80 percent full.

5. Plant Slant

Beans are the cornerstone of most centenarian diets.

6. Wine @ 5

Moderate drinkers outlive non-drinkers — shared with friends and food.

7. Belong

Belonging to a faith-based community adds years to life expectancy.

8. Loved Ones First

Successful centenarians put their families first.

9. Right Tribe

The longest-lived people chose social circles that support healthy behaviours.
